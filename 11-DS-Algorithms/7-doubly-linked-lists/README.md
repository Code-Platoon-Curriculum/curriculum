# Doubly & Circuluar Linked Lists

## What are we trying to accomplish?

Doubly and Circular Linked Lists build on the fundamentals of the Singly Linked List. You will improve your skills in implementing Linked Lists and be better prepared for technical interviews. You will grow your understanding of Computer Science Fundamentals and see some possible applications of the Linked List data structure.
