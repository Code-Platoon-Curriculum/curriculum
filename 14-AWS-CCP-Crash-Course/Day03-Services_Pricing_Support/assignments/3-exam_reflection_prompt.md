# Day 3 - Post-Exam Reflection Prompt

---

You're more than halfway through the certification prep journey. Use this reflection to **self-assess your readiness** and refine your exam strategy.

Answer the following:

1. What kinds of questions are you consistently getting right?
2. Which exam domains (e.g., security, pricing, global infra) still feel uncertain?
3. Can you teach a classmate the difference between IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS? Try writing it out.
4. What distracts or drains you during the test?
5. Do you feel like you're ready to pass this exam today? Why or why not?
