# Training The Model

## What are we trying to accomplish?

By learning the fundamentals of neural network training, you'll understand how to transform raw data into intelligent predictions using PyTorch. This knowledge forms the foundation for building and training AI models that can tackle real-world problems. Through hands-on experience with data loading, training loops, and model optimization, you'll develop the skills needed to create and improve your own neural networks. 🤖
