# AI Chatbots

## What are we trying to accomplish?

In this module, you will build a comprehensive understanding of how conversational AI systems function, from the most basic rule-based models to sophisticated deep learning architectures. Beginning with an *Introduction to Chatbots and Prompt Engineering*, you gain foundational insight into how chatbots interpret and respond to human language, setting the stage for effective interaction design. *Regex and Rule-Based Chatbots* introduces deterministic systems where logic and pattern-matching drive responses, helping you understand the importance of linguistic structure and domain-specific knowledge. As you move into *Text Preprocessing and Language Parsing*, you learn how raw language input is cleaned, tokenized, and structurally analyzed—a crucial step for ensuring accurate interpretation and model performance. In the *Retrieval-Based Chatbots* lecture, you explore systems that select the best response from a pre-defined set, often using embeddings and similarity metrics, bridging the gap between static and learning-based approaches. Finally, *Deep Learning with PyTorch* immerses you in the implementation of neural network-based chatbots, allowing you to leverage data-driven models that learn from conversation history, adapt to context, and generate more natural language responses. Altogether, you’re equipping yourself with both theoretical and practical tools to design, build, and improve chatbots that are intelligent, scalable, and context-aware.
