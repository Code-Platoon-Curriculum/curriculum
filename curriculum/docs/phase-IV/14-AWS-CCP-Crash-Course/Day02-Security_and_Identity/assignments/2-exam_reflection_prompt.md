# Day 2 - Post-Exam Reflection Prompt

---

By now, you've taken your second full AWS Cloud Practitioner practice exam. Let's shift focus toward **recognizing patterns** and building strategy.

Take 10–15 minutes to write brief answers to the following:

1. What services or terminology came up more than once?
2. Were there any areas you *guessed* on that now feel familiar?
3. Can you explain the Shared Responsibility Model in your own words?
4. How did you pace yourself during this exam? Was time a factor?
5. What one mindset or habit could improve your performance tomorrow?

---

> Consider writing these in a personal document you revisit after each exam. The goal is to notice growth and adjust focus — not to be perfect.
