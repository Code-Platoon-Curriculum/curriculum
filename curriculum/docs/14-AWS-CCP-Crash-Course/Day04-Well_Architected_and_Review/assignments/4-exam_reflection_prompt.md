# Day 4 - Post-Exam Reflection Prompt

---

This is your final practice exam before the real thing. Let’s reflect on **exam strategy, confidence, and gaps to close** before test day.

Take 10 minutes and answer:

1. What three concepts do you feel 100% confident explaining to someone else?
2. What remaining blind spots or “trap” questions keep tripping you up?
3. Describe your strategy for handling questions you don’t know right away.
4. If the exam were tomorrow, what’s the **one page or diagram** you’d review again?
5. What advice would you give yourself from Day 1, now that you’ve made it to Day 4?

---

> Think of this as a debrief. Reflecting now can help you avoid surprises and build momentum going into the real exam.
