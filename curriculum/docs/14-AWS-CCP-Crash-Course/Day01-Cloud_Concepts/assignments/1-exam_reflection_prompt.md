# Day 1 - Post-Exam Reflection Prompt

---

You've just completed your first AWS Cloud Practitioner practice exam. Before moving on, take a few minutes to reflect on your experience.

Answer the following questions in your notes or your private journal:

1. Which questions or topics felt the most confusing or unfamiliar?
2. What AWS terms or categories appeared repeatedly?
3. Did any questions surprise you with how they were worded?
4. What is one thing you'll revisit or review again before tomorrow's exam?
5. How would you rate your overall confidence level after this exam (1–10)? Why?

---

> This isn’t graded. It’s just for **you** to identify areas of focus as we progress through the rest of the week.
