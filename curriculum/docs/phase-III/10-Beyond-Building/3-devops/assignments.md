# Assignments

Assignments should be completed from top to bottom as they will raise in difficulty the lower in the stack.

## Part I

- Ensure your application/s is/are ready for deployment. Ensure they are tied to Git Control and or managed by a cloud service. 
    - Front-End: On Github
    - Back-End: On Supabase

## Part II

- Deploy your application, bring them to live and have something to demonstrate.
