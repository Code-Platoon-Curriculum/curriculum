# Machine Learning

## What are we trying to accomplish?

We will deepen our understanding of machine learning by mastering the creation of binary and multi-classification models using PyTorch. This involves learning how to structure, train, and optimize neural networks to solve real-world problems where data needs to be classified into two categories (binary) or multiple categories (multi-class). By building these models, you enhance your ability to work with complex datasets, improve model accuracy, and apply these skills to practical applications, such as predicting student performance or evaluating water potability, which directly ties into your current projects and career growth as a developer.
